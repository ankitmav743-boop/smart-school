## Solution kaise kaam karta hai?

Hamara platform ek complete School Management Portal hai jo 4 logon ko ek jagah connect karta hai — Teacher, Parent/Student, Principal aur AI Assistant.

### Teacher ka kaam:
Teacher sirf apna Teacher ID aur Password daal ke login karta hai. Login hone ke baad uske saamne 1st se 12th tak saari classes ka grid aata hai. Teacher jo bhi class manage karna chahta hai, use click karta hai.

Class select karne ke baad sabse pehle ek "Homework Assign" section aata hai. Teacher usme homework ki description likhta hai, due date set karta hai aur "Assign" button dab deta hai. Bas itna kaam karte hi us class ke har student ko automatically ek notification chali jaati hai ki aaj ka homework yeh hai.

Iske neeche uske class ke saare students ke cards dikhte hain. Har card par student ka naam, SR number aur pichle marks/evaluations dikhte hain. Teacher seedha usi card se kisi bhi student ke marks add kar sakta hai ya "Weekly Evaluation" de sakta hai jisme woh student ki is hafte ki overall performance ko 10 mein se rate karta hai.

### Parent / Student ka kaam:
Parent apne bachche ke naam, SR number aur password se login karta hai. Uske dashboard par yeh sab kuch hota hai:
- Latest homework (notification ke saath)
- Har subject ke marks (exam-wise)
- Teacher ki di hui weekly rating
- Upcoming exam timetable
- AI Study Assistant jo bachche ke marks dekh ke personally batata hai ki kahan mehnat karni chahiye

### Principal ka kaam:
Principal school ke UDISE code aur password se login karta hai. Uska dashboard school-wide data dikhata hai — konsi class ka performance strong hai, konse students weak hain, overall school ki progress kya hai. Yeh sab real-time hota hai bina kisi meeting ya report ke.

### AI Study Assistant:
Yeh feature student ka past performance dekh ke use personalized study tips deta hai. Jaise agar kisi student ke Maths mein marks low hain toh AI bolega "Maths par extra focus karo, yeh chapters dobara padho." Isse har student ko customized guidance milti hai.

### Notification System:
Jab bhi teacher homework assign karta hai ya marks update karta hai, us student ko turant notification milti hai. Is tarah parent ko real-time pata chalta rehta hai ki school mein kya ho raha hai — bina PTM ka intezaar kiye.

Yeh poora system is tarah design kiya gaya hai ki ek teacher 5 minute mein poori class ka kaam kar sake, aur ek parent ghar baithe apne bachche ki puri progress dekh sake.
